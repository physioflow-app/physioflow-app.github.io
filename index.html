Free personalized exercise programs

PhysioFlow helps you build a stretching, strengthening, and mobility routine around the issue you describe. Get a progressive, multi-week program for everyday stiffness or movement limitations in areas such as your neck, shoulders, back, hips, and knees.

How PhysioFlow works

  1. Describe your issue, and optionally your pain level, equipment, and available time.
  2. Review your AI-generated program, step-by-step instructions, and video demonstrations.
  3. Log each session and your pain level. Weeks advance as you hit your session goal, and your progress stays in your browser.

PhysioFlow is free to use and does not require an account. It provides educational exercise guidance and does not diagnose conditions or replace a healthcare professional. Consult a qualified professional before starting an exercise program.
